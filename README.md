One of the most critical missteps is failing to conduct adequate research. Buyers should explore various properties, compare pricing, and assess neighborhood characteristics. This includes checking nearby facilities, transportation options, and overall community vibe.

2. Ignoring Legal Documentation
Skipping the verification of legal documents can lead to major issues post-purchase. Ensure that the property has a clear title and all necessary approvals from local authorities. Consult a legal expert if unsure about documentation requirements.

4. Rushing the Purchase Decision
Purchasing a flat is a significant investment; rushing into a decision often leads to buyer’s remorse. Take the time to weigh options, visit multiple properties, and evaluate their features and pricing thoroughly.
